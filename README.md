# 🍪 Cookies Sales Dashboard

## Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides an overview of a cookie company's sales performance across its business customers. It tracks profitability, sales trends, product performance, customer contributions, and order distribution to support data-driven business decisions.

## Key Metrics

- **Profit Margin:** 58%
- **Total Profit:** $2.72M
- **Total Orders:** 700

---

## Dashboard Features

- KPI cards for Profit Margin, Total Profit, and Total Orders
- Cookies sold by product type
- Profit margin by cookie type
- Orders by city
- Customer order summary
- Customer profit analysis
- Monthly revenue and units sold trend

---

## Results & Insights

- 🍪 **Chocolate Chip** is the best-selling cookie, significantly outperforming other cookie types in units sold.
- 💰 The company generated a total profit of **$2.72M** with an overall **58% profit margin**, indicating strong business profitability.
- 📈 **Snickerdoodle** has the highest profit margin (**63%**), making it the most profitable product despite not having the highest sales volume.
- 🏢 **ACME Bites** is the highest-profit customer, contributing over **$828K** in profit.
- 📦 The business processed **700 customer orders**, with **ACME Bites** placing the highest number of orders.
- 🌍 Order distribution varies across cities, highlighting regions with stronger customer demand.
- 📊 Revenue and units sold fluctuate throughout the year, showing seasonal peaks that can support inventory and sales planning.
